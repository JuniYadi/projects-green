This guide explains how to manage external finance department recipients for automated tax invoices and payment receipts.

---

## 1. Why Configure Billing Contacts?

By default, transactional billing notices are sent only to the organization creator. Adding **Billing Contacts** (`/console/billing/contacts`) ensures all official accounting invoices and receipts are automatically dispatched directly to your accounts payable department.

![Billing Contacts Management](/kb-assets/billing/10-billing-contacts.png)

---

## 2. Adding & Managing Contacts

1. Click **"Add Contact"**.
2. Complete the contact fields:
   - **Name**: Individual name or department label (e.g. _Accounts Payable Department_).
   - **Email**: Active corporate finance address (e.g. *billing@company.com*).
   - **Notification Types**: Choose subscribed message categories (New Invoices, Payment Confirmations, Critical Thresholds).
3. Click **"Save Contact"**.
