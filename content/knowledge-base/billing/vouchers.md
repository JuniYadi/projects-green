This guide explains how to claim and apply promotional vouchers to receive discounts on cloud subscriptions and balance top-ups.

---

## 1. Vouchers Overview

The **Vouchers** page (`/console/billing/vouchers`) manages commercial campaign codes, partnership discounts, and credit grants.

![Promotional Vouchers Management](/kb-assets/billing/09-billing-vouchers.png)

---

## 2. Claiming Promo Codes

1. Obtain a valid promo code from official PFNApp campaigns or enterprise partnership agreements.
2. Enter the alphanumeric code into the **"Claim Voucher"** input.
3. Click **"Claim"**.
4. Once verified, the voucher will appear in your **Active Vouchers** list with its discount percentage/amount and validity countdown.
5. Eligible discounts can be applied directly at checkout when purchasing service subscriptions.
