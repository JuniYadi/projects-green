This guide explains how to browse the PFNApp service catalog, select compute specifications or API quotas, and activate subscription plans for your organization.

---

## 1. Browsing the Service Catalog

The **Services Catalog** (`/console/billing/services`) displays all available cloud computing and API products.

![PFNApp Service Catalog](/kb-assets/billing/05-billing-services.png)

### Available Product Categories:

1. **App Hosting (Container & Web Apps)**:
   - Automated Docker deployments with free SSL, custom domains, and integrated load balancing.
   - Tiered compute configurations from micro containers to dedicated multi-core nodes.
2. **WhatsApp Business Platform**:
   - Broadcast messaging quotas, connected device slots, and automated workflow triggers.
3. **WireGuard Secure VPN**:
   - Encrypted private networking with high-throughput multi-region exit nodes.

---

## 2. Selecting Plans & Checkout

Each service product offers structured subscription tiers:

- **Starter / Entry**: Ideal for development environments and early-stage applications.
- **Pro / Scale**: Optimized for production workloads requiring high throughput and uptime guarantees.
- **Enterprise**: Large-scale compute capacity with custom quota allocations and priority support.

### Ordering Steps:

1. Click **"Select Plan"** or **"Subscribe"** on the target service card.
2. Select the billing term (Monthly or Annual with term discounts).
3. Choose the payment method at checkout: **Organization Balance** or **External Gateway** (Virtual Account, QRIS, Credit Card).
4. Confirm payment to provision and attach the service instantly to your organization.
